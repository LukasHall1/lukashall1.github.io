About Me

My name is Lukas Hall. Software Developer, Game Designer, and Web Developer

I am a passionate software developer with a strong background in game design and web development. I enjoy creating immersive digital experiences that combine technical excellence with creative vision. Throughout my highschool and college experience I have done my best to hone my skills and gain experience in multiple forms of software development to prepare myself as best I can for a career in the industry.

I have always wanted to create videogames that stick with you. My entire purpose for coming into this career and this field is to create a story or an experience that sticks with you. If I can create experiences that makes you feel something, whether that be just simple fun of playing a game to the emotional impact of the story of the experience in a game then I've completed my intention.

When I relax, which should probably be more often, I enjoy playing videogames, picking up a good book, or playing ttrpgs with friends. I have played mostly Dungeons and Dragons but recently I have tried out Pathfinder and am finding it to be a lot of fun. I also have a couple of tabletop miniatures that I enjoy painting for both Warhammer 40k and Warhammer Age of Sigmar. My main armies are about 1000 points of Eldar and I'm just about to hit 2000 points of Dark Angels Space Marines. I haven't played much of the game but I greatly enjoy putting together the miniatures and painting them, and the few games that I have played have been a lot of fun.
